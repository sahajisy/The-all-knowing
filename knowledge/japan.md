## Concepts & ways of living / 生き方・考え方
- **生きがい (ikigai)** — "a reason for being"; the popular 4-circle Venn diagram is a Western mashup, not traditional
- **侘び寂び (wabi-sabi)** — beauty in imperfection and impermanence → arts: Kintsugi, tea ceremony
- **金継ぎ (kintsugi)** — repairing pottery with lacquer and gold; the break becomes part of the history → philosophy: antifragility
- **もったいない (mottainai)** — regret over waste; Wangari Maathai made it a global environmental slogan
- **間 (ma)** — meaningful space/pause; in architecture, music, conversation → arts: John Cage's silence
- **一期一会 (ichigo ichie)** — "one time, one meeting"; from the tea master tradition (Ii Naosuke popularized the phrase)
- **改善 (kaizen)** — continuous small improvement → technology: agile, economics: Toyota
- **森林浴 (shinrin-yoku)** — "forest bathing", term coined 1982 by the Forestry Agency → health
- **根回し (nemawashi)** — originally preparing a tree's roots before transplanting → business: consensus-building
- **本音と建前 (honne / tatemae)** — true feelings vs public face → psychology: social masks
- **おもてなし (omotenashi)** — anticipatory hospitality → business: service design
- **断捨離 (danshari)** & Marie Kondo's ときめき — minimalism as spiritual practice
- **腹八分目 (hara hachi bu)** — eat until 80% full; Okinawan habit → health: Blue Zones
- **守破離 (shu-ha-ri)** — stages of mastery: obey, break, leave → learning theory

## Places with stories / 物語のある場所
- **伊勢神宮** — rebuilt every 20 years (式年遷宮) since ~690 CE; "old" and "new" at once → philosophy: Ship of Theseus
- **法隆寺** — among the world's oldest surviving wooden buildings (~7th century)
- **金剛組** — founded 578 CE, long considered the world's oldest continuously operating company (now part of Takamatsu group) → economics: 老舗 (shinise)
- **軍艦島 (端島)** — once one of the most densely populated places on earth; now ruins, UNESCO-listed
- **直島** — a declining island reborn as an art island (Benesse, Tadao Ando) → arts
- **熊野古道** — pilgrimage routes; one of only two pilgrimage routes on the UNESCO list (with Santiago de Compostela)
- **白川郷** — 合掌造り houses; the steep roofs are designed for heavy snow, the attics raised silkworms
- **屋久島** — cedar forests, 縄文杉 estimated thousands of years old (estimates vary widely)
- **Hokkaido & the Ainu / アイヌ** — Indigenous people, recognized by law in 2019; Upopoy museum (2020)
- **沖縄・琉球王国** — an independent kingdom with its own culture and trade network until 1879

## Food / 食
- **うま味 (umami)** — identified by Kikunae Ikeda (池田菊苗) in 1908 from kombu dashi → science: glutamate receptors
- **寿司の起源** — narezushi: fish preserved in fermented rice, originally from Southeast Asia; nigiri is Edo-era fast food
- **天ぷら** — Portuguese influence (16th c.) → history: Nanban trade
- **カレーライス** — came via the British Navy; the Japanese Navy's Friday curry tradition
- **インスタントラーメン** — Momofuku Ando (安藤百福), Chicken Ramen, 1958 → technology: food engineering
- **発酵文化** — miso, shoyu, natto, sake, katsuobushi (one of the hardest foods in the world, mold-fermented)
- **駅弁 (ekiben)** — regional railway lunch boxes as local culture

## Society & modern life / 社会
- **少子高齢化** — Japan as the world's preview of aging societies; ~29% aged 65+
- **14,125 islands** — the 2023 GSI recount (previously 6,852, from a 1987 Coast Guard count)
- **Punctuality** — the famous 2017 apology for a train leaving 20 seconds early (Tsukuba Express)
- **自動販売機** — ~4 million vending machines (density among the highest in the world)
- **コンビニ** — infrastructure: bills, parcels, disaster support
- **Earthquake engineering / 耐震** — base isolation (免震), Tokyo Skytree's central pillar inspired by five-story pagodas (五重塔の心柱)
- **Galápagos syndrome / ガラパゴス化** — technologies that evolved uniquely in Japan (feature phones) → technology: i-mode
- **Showa Retro / 昭和レトロ & City Pop revival** — how the world rediscovered 1980s Japanese music → arts

## History hooks / 歴史の小ネタ
- **鎖国 (sakoku)** — not total isolation: Dejima (Dutch), Tsushima (Korea), Satsuma (Ryukyu), Matsumae (Ainu) "four gates"
- **蘭学 (Rangaku)** — Dutch learning; 解体新書 (1774) translated anatomy → language: coined medical words
- **明治維新** — astonishingly fast modernization; Iwakura Mission (1871–73) toured the US and Europe for ~2 years
- **江戸** — one of the world's largest cities (~1 million) in the 18th century, with remarkable recycling (古着, 灰買い)
- **寺子屋** — widespread basic literacy in the Edo period
