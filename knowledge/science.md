# Science / 科学

## Physics & the universe
- **Entropy / エントロピー** — why time seems to have a direction; also a measure of information → technology: Shannon
- **Relativity** — GPS satellites must correct for both special and general relativity (~38 microseconds/day) or positions drift by kilometers
- **Quantum mechanics** — superposition, entanglement, uncertainty; the double-slit experiment
- **Neutrinos & Kamiokande / カミオカンデ** — Koshiba (2002 Nobel) and Kajita (2015 Nobel) at Super-Kamiokande, a tank of 50,000 tons of ultra-pure water under a mine in Gifu
- **Dark matter & dark energy** — ~95% of the universe's energy content is not ordinary matter
- **Yukawa Hideki / 湯川秀樹** — predicted the meson (1935); Japan's first Nobel laureate (1949)
- **Nihonium (Nh, 113)** — first element discovered in Asia, at RIKEN (理研), named 2016

## Life
- **Evolution by natural selection** — Darwin and Wallace (1858); Galápagos finches → japan.md: ガラパゴス化
- **DNA** — double helix (1953), Rosalind Franklin's Photo 51
- **CRISPR** — a bacterial immune system turned into gene-editing scissors; the repeats were first noticed by Yoshizumi Ishino's team in Osaka (1987)
- **iPS cells / iPS細胞** — Shinya Yamanaka (山中伸弥), 2006; adult cells reprogrammed with four factors (2012 Nobel)
- **Autophagy / オートファジー** — Yoshinori Ohsumi (大隅良典), 2016 Nobel; cells recycling themselves → japan.md: mottainai
- **Microbiome** — trillions of microbes; gut–brain axis → health
- **Tardigrades / クマムシ** — survive vacuum, radiation, dehydration (as "tuns")
- **Slime molds / 粘菌** — solved a Tokyo rail network design problem (Nakagaki et al., 2010); Minakata Kumagusu (南方熊楠) studied them → technology: network optimization
- **Convergent evolution** — eyes, wings, crab-like bodies ("carcinization") evolved independently many times

## Earth
- **Plate tectonics** — Japan sits where four plates meet; accepted only in the 1960s (Wegener proposed drift in 1912)
- **Kuroshio / 黒潮** — one of the strongest ocean currents; shapes Japan's climate and fisheries
- **Fossa Magna / フォッサマグナ** — the great rift dividing NE and SW Japan; also roughly the 50Hz/60Hz power split (historical, from German vs American generators) → technology
- **Carbon cycle & climate** — CO₂ ~280 ppm pre-industrial vs 420+ ppm today
- **Snow crystals / 雪の結晶** — Ukichiro Nakaya (中谷宇吉郎) made the first artificial snow crystals (1936): "Snow is a letter from the sky" (雪は天から送られた手紙である)

## Mathematics
- **Gödel's incompleteness** — any consistent formal system rich enough for arithmetic has true but unprovable statements
- **Wasan / 和算** — Edo-period Japanese math; Seki Takakazu (関孝和) found determinants around the same time as Leibniz; 算額 (math tablets) offered at shrines
- **Fermat's Last Theorem** — proved by Andrew Wiles (1994) building on the Taniyama–Shimura conjecture (谷山・志村予想)
- **Bayes' theorem** — updating beliefs with evidence → philosophy: rationality
- **Fractals** — coastlines, ferns, Hokusai's waves → arts
- **Game theory** — prisoner's dilemma, Nash equilibrium → economics

## Hooks
- Bananas, Brazil nuts and the human body are all slightly radioactive (potassium-40, radium) — completely harmless at normal amounts.
- Octopuses have about two-thirds of their neurons in their arms.
- There are more possible orderings of a deck of cards (52! ≈ 8×10⁶⁷) than there are atoms on Earth (~10⁵⁰).
- Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach Earth.
