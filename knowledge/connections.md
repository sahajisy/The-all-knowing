# Connections / つながり — the "そういえば…" map

Cross-domain links that make conversations come alive. Each line is a ready-made
"that reminds me of…" jump. Sensei adds new ones during `/wrap`.

## Technology ↔ Japan
- **Kanban (Toyota) ↔ Kanban boards & TCP backpressure** — "pull" systems: downstream signals when it's ready for more
- **Go (囲碁) ↔ QR codes ↔ AlphaGo** — Hara's QR inspiration was Go stones; Go later became AI's landmark challenge (AlphaGo, 2016)
- **i-mode ↔ emoji ↔ Unicode** — a Japanese carrier feature became a universal language
- **Galápagos syndrome ↔ Darwin's finches ↔ technology lock-in** — isolation drives unique evolution, in biology and in phones
- **Shinkansen ↔ kingfisher beak ↔ biomimicry** — the 500 Series nose was redesigned after a kingfisher to reduce tunnel boom
- **Ise Jingū rebuilding ↔ Ship of Theseus ↔ software rewrites** — identity through continuous replacement
- **Kaizen ↔ agile retrospectives ↔ compound interest** — small improvements accumulate exponentially

## Science ↔ Everyday life
- **Umami ↔ glutamate receptors ↔ MSG controversy** — a 1908 Tokyo discovery and a later (largely unfounded) Western scare
- **Slime mold ↔ Tokyo rail network ↔ internet routing** — decentralized optimization without a brain
- **Snow crystals (Nakaya) ↔ weather forecasting ↔ "a letter from the sky"** — reading the atmosphere's history in a flake
- **Autophagy ↔ mottainai ↔ garbage collection in programming** — recycling what's no longer needed
- **Entropy ↔ information ↔ tidy rooms** — why disorder is the default, and why compression works

## History ↔ Today
- **Dojima rice futures ↔ modern derivatives ↔ candlestick charts** — Osaka merchants invented tools traders use today
- **Sputnik ↔ DARPA ↔ the internet** — a Cold War shock created the network you're using now
- **Dutch learning (Rangaku) ↔ 解体新書 ↔ Japanese medical vocabulary** — translation as a nation-building technology
- **Columbian Exchange ↔ chili in kimchi ↔ tempura** — global trade quietly rewrote "traditional" cuisines
- **Edo recycling ↔ circular economy** — a closed-loop city of a million people, centuries before the term

## Philosophy ↔ Practice
- **Stoicism ↔ Morita therapy ↔ CBT/ACT** — East and West arriving at similar tools for the mind
- **Wabi-sabi ↔ kintsugi ↔ antifragility (Taleb)** — beauty and strength from damage
- **Shu-ha-ri ↔ Dreyfus model of skill ↔ learning to code** — the universal path from rules to intuition
- **Ma (間) ↔ John Cage ↔ whitespace in design** — the power of emptiness
- **Goodhart's law ↔ Soviet nail factories ↔ AI reward hacking ↔ exam cramming** — optimizing the metric, missing the point

## Arts ↔ Economics
- **Ukiyo-e as packing paper ↔ Japonisme ↔ Van Gogh** — cheap mass media became high art abroad
- **Hanafuda ↔ Nintendo ↔ Pokémon** — a 19th-century card company's path to a global franchise
- **City Pop ↔ YouTube algorithms ↔ nostalgia economy** — how recommendation engines revive forgotten art

## Language ↔ Everything
- **Meiji coinages (経済, 哲学) ↔ modern Chinese vocabulary** — Japan exported words back to the language it borrowed its script from
- **"Computer" was a job ↔ Hidden Figures ↔ AI and jobs today** — every automation renames work
- **Tycoon (大君) ↔ shogun diplomacy ↔ American business slang** — a word's journey across the Pacific
