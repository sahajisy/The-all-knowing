# Arts & Culture / 芸術・文化

## Visual art
- **Ukiyo-e / 浮世絵** — mass-produced woodblock prints; arrived in Europe partly as packing material and sparked Japonisme
- **Hokusai's Great Wave / 神奈川沖浪裏** — uses Prussian blue, an imported synthetic pigment → science: chemistry
- **Van Gogh & Japan** — copied Hiroshige prints; dreamed of Arles as "the Japan of the South"
- **Impressionism** — painting light; made possible partly by paint in tubes (1841)
- **Perspective** — Brunelleschi's experiment (Florence, c. 1415)
- **Kintsugi, Mingei / 民藝** — Yanagi Sōetsu's celebration of everyday folk craft → japan.md
- **Yayoi Kusama / 草間彌生** — polka dots, infinity rooms

## Architecture & design
- **Katsura Imperial Villa / 桂離宮** — admired by Bruno Taut as the essence of modern simplicity
- **Metabolism / メタボリズム** — 1960s Japanese movement; Nakagin Capsule Tower (Kisho Kurokawa), demolished 2022
- **Tadao Ando / 安藤忠雄** — self-taught former boxer; concrete and light (Church of the Light)
- **Bauhaus** — form follows function; influenced and was influenced by Japanese design
- **Muji / 無印良品** — "no brand" as a brand; Kenya Hara (原研哉) and "emptiness"
- **Tatami proportions** — rooms measured in mats (畳) → mathematics of modular design

## Music
- **Equal temperament** — the compromise that lets pianos play in every key
- **Pentatonic scales** — found across Japanese, Scottish, Chinese, West African music
- **City Pop** — Mariya Takeuchi's "Plastic Love" became a global internet phenomenon decades later
- **YMO / イエロー・マジック・オーケストラ** — pioneers of electronic and synth-pop music; influenced techno and hip-hop
- **Video game music** — Koji Kondo (Super Mario), Nobuo Uematsu; now played by orchestras
- **John Cage's 4′33″** — "silence" filled with ambient sound → japan.md: 間; Cage was influenced by Zen

## Literature & story
- **The Tale of Genji / 源氏物語** — Murasaki Shikibu, ~1000 CE, often called the first novel
- **Haiku** — Bashō's frog; 5-7-5 in Japanese "on" (morae), not syllables
- **Murakami Haruki** — translated into 50+ languages; jazz bar owner before novelist
- **Hero's journey (Campbell)** — the pattern behind myths and Star Wars
- **Rashomon effect** — Kurosawa's film gave a name to contradictory testimonies → philosophy: perspective

## Film, anime, games
- **Kurosawa** — Seven Samurai remade as The Magnificent Seven; Star Wars borrowed from The Hidden Fortress
- **Studio Ghibli** — hand-drawn craft, environmental themes
- **Anime production committees / 製作委員会** — how anime is financed → economics
- **Nintendo** — founded 1889 as a hanafuda (花札) card maker
- **Pokémon** — Satoshi Tajiri's childhood insect collecting
