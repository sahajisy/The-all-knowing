# Economics & Business / 経済・ビジネス

## Core ideas
- **Supply & demand, opportunity cost** — the price of anything is what you give up for it
- **Comparative advantage (Ricardo, 1817)** — trade benefits both sides even if one is better at everything
- **Invisible hand (Adam Smith)** — mentioned only a few times in his work; he also wrote on moral sentiments
- **Inflation & deflation** — Japan's "lost decades" (失われた30年) and deflationary mindset
- **Tragedy of the commons** — and Elinor Ostrom's Nobel-winning work showing communities can manage commons (e.g. Japanese 入会地, iriaichi, village commons)
- **Network effects** — a phone is useless if only you have one → technology: platforms
- **Creative destruction (Schumpeter)** — innovation destroys old industries
- **Moral hazard** — insured people take more risk → 2008 crisis

## Japan & business
- **Toyota Production System / トヨタ生産方式** — just-in-time, jidoka (自働化), andon cords; Taiichi Ohno was partly inspired by American supermarkets → technology: kanban
- **Keiretsu / 系列** — networks of interlinked companies
- **Shinise / 老舗** — Japan has a very large share of the world's companies over 100 and 200 years old
- **Lifetime employment / 終身雇用** — origins mostly postwar, and changing now
- **Sogo shosha / 総合商社** — trading houses like Mitsubishi Corp.; Warren Buffett invested in five of them (2020)
- **Dojima Rice Exchange / 堂島米市場 (1730)** — often cited as the world's first organized futures market → finance
- **Candlestick charts / ローソク足** — traditionally attributed to rice trader Homma Munehisa (本間宗久)
- **Plaza Accord (1985)** — yen appreciation → bubble economy → collapse

## Money & finance
- **Compound interest** — the "rule of 72": years to double ≈ 72 / interest rate (%)
- **Index funds** — John Bogle; most active managers underperform over the long run
- **Behavioral economics** — loss aversion, nudges (Thaler)
- **Cryptocurrency & blockchain** — Bitcoin whitepaper (2008) by pseudonymous "Satoshi Nakamoto" → technology: distributed consensus
- **Yap stone money** — giant stones whose ownership changed without moving: an early "ledger" → blockchain

## Management & work
- **Peter Drucker** — hugely popular in Japan (もしドラ)
- **OKRs** — from Intel (Andy Grove) to Google
- **Jobs to be done** — people "hire" products to do a job (Clayton Christensen)
- **Innovator's dilemma** — why great companies miss disruption
- **Parkinson's law** — work expands to fill the time available
