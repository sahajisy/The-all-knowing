# Health & Lifestyle / 健康・暮らし

(Educational conversation only — not medical advice.)

## Body & mind
- **Sleep** — memory consolidation; circadian rhythm (2017 Nobel); naps (昼寝) and 居眠り (inemuri) culture in Japan
- **Exercise** — walking and longevity; radio taiso (ラジオ体操, since 1928) as national habit
- **Stress** — acute vs chronic; cortisol
- **Neuroplasticity** — the brain rewires with learning at any age
- **Spaced repetition / 間隔反復** — Ebbinghaus's forgetting curve (1885); this bot's `/quiz` uses it
- **Habit loop** — cue, routine, reward; small habits (Atomic Habits)
- **Karoshi / 過労死** — a Japanese word that entered other languages → economics: work culture

## Food & nutrition
- **Washoku / 和食** — UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage (2013)
- **Ichiju-sansai / 一汁三菜** — one soup, three dishes: balance as structure
- **Fermented foods** — natto (vitamin K2), miso, kimchi, yogurt → science: microbiome
- **Mediterranean diet** — and comparisons with Okinawan diet
- **Salt** — Japan's challenge: high sodium (soy sauce, miso, pickles)
- **Green tea / 緑茶** — catechins, theanine; Eisai (栄西) brought tea culture from China (1191)

## Longevity
- **Japan's life expectancy** — among the highest in the world (~87 for women, ~81 for men)
- **Okinawa Centenarian Study** — started in 1975; diet, community (moai / 模合), purpose
- **Hara hachi bu / 腹八分目** — calorie restriction research in animals
- **Social connection** — loneliness as a health risk; Japan and the UK appointed ministers for loneliness

## Lifestyle design
- **Minimalism** — Marie Kondo, danshari → japan.md
- **Deep work** — Cal Newport; focus as a superpower → technology: attention economy
- **Pomodoro technique** — Francesco Cirillo's tomato-shaped kitchen timer
- **Onsen culture / 温泉** — ~3,000 onsen areas; balneology → science: geothermal
- **Sauna (Finland) & サ活** — Japan's sauna boom, "totonou" (ととのう)
