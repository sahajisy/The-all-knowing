# History / 歴史

## Big turning points
- **Agricultural revolution** — ~12,000 years ago; Göbekli Tepe (Turkey) temple may predate farming
- **Writing** — invented independently in Mesopotamia, Egypt, China, and Mesoamerica → language.md
- **Axial Age** — ~800–200 BCE: Confucius, Buddha, Socrates, Hebrew prophets roughly in the same era → philosophy
- **Silk Road** — not one road but networks; Shōsōin (正倉院) in Nara holds treasures from Persia via the Silk Road → japan.md
- **Printing press (Gutenberg, c. 1450)** — but movable type existed earlier in China (Bi Sheng) and Korea (Jikji, 1377)
- **Columbian Exchange** — tomatoes to Italy, chili peppers to Asia, potatoes to Europe; changed every cuisine
- **Industrial Revolution** — coal, steam, factories; why it began in Britain is still debated
- **The Meiji Restoration** — a feudal country industrialized in a generation → japan.md

## Forgotten or surprising stories
- **Mongol invasions of Japan (1274, 1281)** — the "kamikaze" typhoons; underwater archaeology at Takashima found the fleet
- **Hasekura Tsunenaga / 支倉常長** — samurai envoy who reached Spain and Rome (1613–1620); some Spaniards named "Japón" in Coria del Río may be descendants
- **Yasuke / 弥助** — an African man who served Oda Nobunaga
- **John Manjiro / ジョン万次郎** — shipwrecked fisherman raised in the US who later helped Japan's opening
- **Sugihara Chiune / 杉原千畝** — issued thousands of transit visas to Jewish refugees in Lithuania (1940)
- **The Great Stink (London, 1858)** — smell of the Thames forced the creation of a modern sewer system
- **Tulip mania (1630s)** — often cited as the first speculative bubble (modern historians think it was smaller than legend) → economics
- **Library of Alexandria** — declined over centuries rather than one dramatic fire
- **The Little Ice Age** — climate cooling that shaped famines, revolutions, and Edo-period famines (天明の大飢饉)

## Empires & systems
- **Rome** — roads, law, concrete (Roman concrete self-heals with lime clasts)
- **Tang dynasty China** — model for Nara/Heian Japan: city grid of Heian-kyō copied Chang'an
- **Ottoman Empire** — 600 years bridging Europe and Asia
- **British Empire & tea** — opium wars, India, and why the British drink tea → economics: trade
- **Cold War & space race** — Sputnik (1957) led to DARPA and, eventually, the internet → technology

## Thinking about history
- **Hindsight bias** — the past always looks inevitable afterward → philosophy
- **"History doesn't repeat, but it rhymes"** — commonly attributed to Mark Twain, but no evidence he said it
- **Microhistory** — big stories through one village, object, or person (e.g. "Salt", "Cod" by Mark Kurlansky)
