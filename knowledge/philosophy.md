# Philosophy & Psychology / 哲学・心理学

## Classic ideas
- **Socratic method** — knowledge through questioning (this bot's own style!)
- **Plato's cave** — mistaking shadows for reality → technology: filter bubbles, VR
- **Ship of Theseus** — if every plank is replaced, is it the same ship? → japan.md: Ise Jingū rebuilding
- **Stoicism** — Epictetus (a former slave) and Marcus Aurelius (an emperor): control what you can
- **Zen / 禅** — Dōgen (道元), "just sitting" (只管打坐); D.T. Suzuki (鈴木大拙) introduced Zen to the West
- **Kyoto School / 京都学派** — Nishida Kitarō (西田幾多郎) and "pure experience"; the 哲学の道 is named after his walks
- **Confucianism** — relationships, ritual, self-cultivation; deep influence on East Asian workplaces
- **Buddhist impermanence / 諸行無常** — opening of the Heike Monogatari (祇園精舎の鐘の声…)
- **Existentialism** — Sartre: "existence precedes essence"; Camus and the absurd (Sisyphus)
- **Utilitarianism vs deontology** — the trolley problem → technology: self-driving cars

## Thinking tools
- **Occam's razor** — prefer simpler explanations
- **First-principles thinking** — reason from fundamentals rather than analogy
- **Falsifiability (Karl Popper)** — a scientific claim must be testable/refutable
- **Paradigm shifts (Thomas Kuhn)** — science changes in revolutions, not only increments
- **Steelmanning** — argue against the strongest version of an opposing view
- **Inversion** — "tell me where I'll die, so I won't go there" (Charlie Munger)
- **Map vs territory** — models are not reality

## Psychology & behavior
- **Cognitive biases** — confirmation bias, anchoring, availability heuristic (Kahneman & Tversky)
- **System 1 / System 2** — fast intuition vs slow reasoning
- **Dunning–Kruger effect** — popular version overstated; the original finding is more modest
- **Flow / フロー** — Csikszentmihalyi; challenge matches skill → japan.md: 職人
- **Growth mindset** — Carol Dweck; replication results are mixed, worth discussing critically
- **Replication crisis** — many famous findings (ego depletion, power posing) didn't hold up → science: method
- **Amae / 甘え** — Takeo Doi (土居健郎) on dependency as a key concept of Japanese psychology
- **Morita therapy / 森田療法** — Shoma Morita (1919): accept feelings, act anyway → Stoicism, ACT
- **Naikan / 内観** — Japanese self-reflection method: what did I receive, give, and trouble others with?
- **Hedonic treadmill** — happiness tends to return to a baseline
