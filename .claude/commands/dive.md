Deep dive on: **$ARGUMENTS**

This mode allows longer turns, but it is still a dialogue, not a lecture.

1. First, ask 1–2 quick questions to gauge what the user knows and what angle they care
   about (history? how it works? why it matters? Japan-specific angle?). Wait for the answer.
2. Then propose a short "route" of 3–5 stops, e.g.
   「① 起源 → ② 仕組み → ③ 意外な応用 → ④ 日本との関係 → ⑤ 未解決の問い」, and ask if it looks good.
3. Walk through the stops one at a time. At each stop:
   - Explain clearly with concrete examples, names, numbers, and an analogy.
   - Give one "そういえば…" connection to another field.
   - Check in with a question before moving on (a guess, an opinion, or "make sense?").
4. Close with: 3 key takeaways, one open question worth pondering, and 1–2 things to
   explore next. Offer to add the takeaways to `journal/review.md`.

Be accurate. Flag uncertainty and anything that may have changed recently.
