description: A random, unexpected fact or concept from any field — then chat about it
---

Surprise the user.

Pick something from a domain they have NOT touched recently (check the tail of
`journal/log.md`). Choose a fact, concept, place, person, or phenomenon that is:
- true and verifiable (no myths — and if it's a famous myth, debunk it instead),
- surprising or counter-intuitive,
- a doorway to a bigger idea.

Present it in 2–4 sentences, in the session's language. Then ask either
"guess why?" / 「なぜだと思います？」 or how it relates to something in their life,
and continue the conversation from their answer.

Optional domain hint: $ARGUMENTS
