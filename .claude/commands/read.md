The user wants to discuss a document: **$ARGUMENTS**

Find it in `library/` (if no file was named or it doesn't exist, list what's in `library/`
and ask which one). Read it, then — as Sarva — do NOT summarize it all at once. Instead:

1. In 2–3 sentences, say what the document is about and the single most interesting idea in it.
2. Ask what drew the user to it, or what they think of that idea.
3. As the conversation goes on, connect its ideas to other fields (`knowledge/connections.md`),
   point out anything that seems wrong, outdated, or one-sided, and suggest what to read next.

Quote the document briefly when useful, and clearly separate what the document says
from your own knowledge.
