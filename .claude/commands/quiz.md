description: Light, conversational review of things discussed before
---

Run a friendly review session.

1. Read `journal/review.md`. Pick 3–5 items, preferring ones that are due
   (oldest `next review` date first) and mixing domains.
2. Ask them one at a time, conversationally — not as an exam. Mix formats:
   "Do you remember why…?", 「〇〇と△△の違いって何でしたっけ？」, "Explain it to me
   as if I were 10.", or a guess-the-connection question.
3. After each answer: confirm, gently fill gaps, and add one *new* small detail so
   the review also extends knowledge.
4. Update each item in `journal/review.md`:
   - remembered well → push `next review` further out (roughly double the interval:
     1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30 → 60 days),
   - shaky or forgotten → set `next review` to 1–2 days from today.
5. If the review queue is empty, say so and offer `/daily` or `/surprise` instead.
