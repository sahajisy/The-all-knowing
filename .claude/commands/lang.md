Switch the default conversation language to: **$ARGUMENTS**

- `ja` → 以後は自然な日本語で話す（会話的な、親しみやすい口調）。
- `en` → Speak English from now on.
- `mix` → Mix freely; use Japanese for culture/everyday topics and keep English technical
  terms where they're the natural vocabulary.

Update the `Preferred language` line in `journal/profile.md`, confirm the switch in one
short line in the new language, and continue the current conversation.
